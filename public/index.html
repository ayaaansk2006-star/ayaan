Questions? Answered.

Why can't I buy the Triumph Xpulse-type GPS mount alone?

That mount needs minor modifications while installing the Hero Xpulse 210 windshield, so it's sold only as a kit with the windshield.

Where do I get the RE Himalayan 450 Touring windshield?

Buy it from any Royal Enfield dealership, or WhatsApp us and we'll supply it.

How do I pay?

At checkout, scan the UPI QR with any UPI app — the amount is filled in automatically. Then enter the transaction ID (UTR) shown after payment.

How do I know my order went through?

You get an order ID instantly and email updates as your order moves. You can check anytime on the Track Order page.
